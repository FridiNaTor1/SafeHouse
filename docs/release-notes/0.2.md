A big update to the launcher, the native Sly 2 port and the Sly 3 port in development, with a lot more platforms. You still need your own copy of the games.

## Highlights
- **Vulkan renderer** for Sly 2, now the default on Windows, Linux (SteamOS) and Android, with OpenGL fallback
- **VR through OpenXR** on Meta Quest, the Steam Frame and PC headsets, with an experimental **First Person** mode
- **New platforms:** Android (phones and Meta Quest), iOS / iPadOS (sideloaded), **Nintendo Switch 2**, Windows on ARM, Linux aarch64
- **Korean Sly 2 disc** supported, and the launcher in **15 languages**
- **Collection Style Launcher:** The Sly Collection's own game-select menu, from your disc
- **Level editor** with a **cutscene editor**, plus a **translation and dubbing tool**
- **Sly 3 (in development)** on every platform, with a lot more working, including new multiplayer options

## Renderer and performance
- Fewer hitches the first time a scene appears
- Less GPU work on phones, Quest and Steam Frame (tile GPU optimisations)
- **FSR 1 upscaling** (off by default, in Display settings), also on iOS and macOS
- Texture packs and HD textures load on other cores
- **HUD Placement** (Sly 2 and Sly 3): 4:3 (default), 16:9, 16:10 or Match Screen, even at 32:9
- **Monitor option:** pick which display the game uses

## VR (Sly 2)
- PC headsets and the Steam Frame through OpenXR, "Play in VR" in the launcher when a headset is found
- **Meta Quest** on Vulkan with multiview, foveation and dynamic resolution
- **First Person (Experimental)** by cooperbooperscooper: eyes at the character's head, the cane in your right hand, snap turn
- World Scale and Outline Thickness options
- The Controls page shows the VR controllers you're holding (Quest, Index, Steam Frame, WMR, Vive, Reverb G2, Pico)
- Cutscenes play in VR with a subtitle panel that follows your head, binocucom talks in a frame in the room, the Episode 2 dance and hacking playable in VR
- Fixes: the Quest freezing while recording, flashlight beams in both eyes, fades, sharp binocucom text, Quit to Desktop ending VR properly

## Android, Meta Quest and iOS
- **Android:** the launcher and Sly 2, on-screen controls, vibration, home-screen shortcuts, works on Android 17, a Mali shader crash fixed (by fyrnas)
- New APKs install over the old one and keep your data; the launcher checks for updates
- **iOS / iPadOS:** the launcher and Sly 2 on Metal, sideloaded (AltStore / SideStore / Sideloadly), ProMotion up to 120 Hz

## Nintendo Switch and Switch 2
- **Switch 2 build** (`SafeHouse-NX2.nro`, for kaiHEN homebrew) by NaGaa95
- Switch installs from USB drives and FAT32 cards (split .iso and CHD), by NaGaa95

## Launcher
- Collection Style Launcher: touch support, an aspect ratio option, a smooth hand-off into Sly 2 and back, add it to Steam or as a shortcut
- **Install folders** and a **Disc images folder** (with maxigasparini)
- A Quit button for Steam Big Picture / game mode
- A new credits list with developer pictures (thematuu, NaGaa95, cooperbooperscooper, fyrnas, maxigasparini)
- **Windows:** a per-user installer without admin rights; installs work in deep folders (long paths, OneDrive / antivirus retries, clear error messages) and use shorter folder names

## Level editor (desktop)
- An outliner with types and groups, hide / isolate, search, multi-select, move / rotate / scale with snapping
- Cameras with a live preview, play-test a job or from the view
- Brushes with collision, an asset browser for every level's assets, guard patrols, traversal and interactables (with who can use them: Sly / Bentley / Murray)
- Map states (play every state of a level, make your own) and brand-new levels; every object and setting editable
- **Cutscene editor:** every scene as a timeline, scrubbing with ghosts, editing, new cutscenes, character paths, recorded play sessions, and a custom caption / title toggle for mod cutscenes; the preview draws every character the game shows
- **Readable scripts:** everything named, compiler-style errors and warnings with quick fixes
- **Character export for Blender** (GLB), posed as in the game; the animations play with Blender's default import
- Debug overlays: hidden objects, guard paths and vision, triggers, collision, cameras, stats

## Languages
- The launcher in 15 languages (Korean added)
- The **Korean Sly 2 disc** installs and plays; Japanese and Korean text on the USA / European discs
- Languages from any installed region, with separate text and voice languages
- **Translation and dubbing tool:** lines grouped by episode and character, button codes shown as icons, recording in the tool (a countdown, the original playing along, trimming on a waveform), an automatic radio effect for binocucom lines, exports with your dubs (as FLAC, optionally a .zip)
- Curly apostrophes no longer show as "?"
- Font fixes for Faroese, Icelandic and others (ð, í ì î ï)

## Controllers
- Any controller works; GameCube, Switch 2, Wii, Wii U, Quest and Steam Frame prompts
- An in-game Remap page, and every Sly 2 option in the launcher

## Sly 2 fixes and extras
- **Cutscene skip:** still a 2-second hold by default (Instant Skip for one press), multi-part scenes skip in one go, a faster fast-forward, no freeze on job-ending scenes
- **Vaults:** the power-up is no longer lost when quitting mid-job, and stuck saves get it back
- **Gadget menu** (by thematuu): closing it with Triangle no longer attacks
- Sounds no longer cut out in busy areas (Episode 8's blimp)
- Fixes from thematuu: guard pathfinding, Trojan Tank spider health, pickpocketing, ledges, Clockwerk disappearing, the Blimp HQ key softlock and more
- Cheat codes, audio / microphone device selection, the USB headset feature (shouting alerts guards)

## Sly 3 (in development)
Still marked "in development": it may not be fully playable yet, but a lot has changed.
- **The Prologue can be finished** from start to end
- **Two full audits** against the finished Sly 3 decompilation: about 180 differences fixed
- **Characters:** Sly 3's own move set; Sly's attacks have their sounds, the air slam and the air attack work as the original; the Panda King playable; Bentley's Grapple-Cam; Murray rows the boat
- **Guards and missions:** pathfinding, alerts, follow missions, disguises, Carmelita's lock-on, the original's level reset after a death
- **Modes:** diving and swimming, the RC car, the jolly boat, turrets, safe dials and code panels, launch pads
- **Picture:** HUD, binocucom, smoke, water, particles; the original's red/cyan 3D mode (off by default, a toggle in the launcher)
- **Renderer at Sly 2's level:** Vulkan, tile GPU optimisations, FSR 1, HUD Placement
- **Every platform:** Windows, Linux, macOS, Android and Quest (flat), iOS, Switch and Switch 2
- The title says "Resume Game" when Start continues your save, and saves resume at their checkpoint

## Sly 3 multiplayer (PC extras; the original two-player modes stay the default)
- **Cops and Robbers:** a map select with every hub including **Kaine Island**, **2 vs 2** teams, winner screens on every map
- **Hackathon:** a character select with the real hacking tanks, **3-4 players** with a chained laser, four exit docks and four health gauges; plays from round 1 to the end
- **Biplane Duel:** a 3-4 player free-for-all
- **Galleon Duel:** the two-player duel plays to a winner, plus a **3-4 player** free-for-all: the Guru with Dimitri at the helm, and the Panda King with **Jing King's shadow** steering from behind a paper screen booth; sunk players watch the battle
