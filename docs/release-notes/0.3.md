A big update: Sly 1 rebuilt on the latest ProjectCane and brought up to Sly 2's level on every platform, a round of Android improvements for all the games, and a lot of Sly 2 fixes. You still need your own copy of the games.

## Highlights
- **Sly 1 rebuilt** on ProjectCane 0.5.0 by theclub654, now on **every platform**: Windows, Linux, macOS, Android and Meta Quest, iOS / iPadOS, Nintendo Switch and Switch 2
- **Native Vulkan** for Sly 1, like Sly 2, with OpenGL (and OpenGL ES on Android) as a fallback
- Sly 1 gets Sly 2's quality-of-life features: keyboard and mouse, controller icons, high refresh rates, super ultrawide, a debug menu
- **Every region's Sly 1 disc:** USA, Europe, Japan and Korea, and the **PS3 Sly Collection HD textures**
- **Android:** Turnip drivers, updates inside the launcher, install and save locations like on PC, Meta Quest 2, older Android versions
- **Freeze camera and time controls** in all three games' debug menus, for screenshots and videos

## Sly 1
- Rebuilt on ProjectCane 0.5.0 by theclub654, kept as its own repository so new ProjectCane updates come in easily
- **Native Vulkan** with OpenGL fallback; Metal on macOS / iOS; the Switch renderer; OpenGL ES on Android when Vulkan isn't available
- **Every region's disc** (USA, Europe, Japan, Korea), installed by the launcher, with every language of the disc
- **PS3 Sly Collection HD textures** (colours matched, so eyes keep their colour)
- **Keyboard and mouse** with the same layout as Sly 2 and Sly 3, remappable in the game and in the launcher
- **Controller button icons:** Xbox, Steam Controller, Steam Deck, Nintendo Switch, PS5, PS4, PS3 or the original PS2 icons, switching with the device you use, including the rhythm game's notes and the tutorial's stick icons (WASD and the mouse on keyboard)
- **Rhythm game keys** on keyboard: W, A, D and Space by default, remappable
- **Frame rates:** 30, 60, 90, 120, 144, 240 FPS and uncapped
- **Super ultrawide** up to 32:9 with **HUD Placement**; binocucom talk windows that use the wider screen
- **Settings.ini** like Sly 2, with **Audio Output** device selection on desktop
- **Hold to skip** cutscenes and binocucom talks (2 s with a ring), or **Instant Skip**
- **Debug menu** like Sly 2's (Debug mode, F10): warps, noclip, free camera, teleports and more
- Bigger option pages that scroll calmly with the mouse
- Fixes:
  - Title screen music
  - Wrong sound effects on USA installs (episode sound banks mixed up), by razziedazzel; existing installs are fixed without reinstalling
  - A crash when going through a door while a charm swirls (ProjectCane #57)
  - A loud howl in High Class Heist's water section on Windows (ProjectCane #49)
  - Crash reports and error messages instead of silent exits

## Sly 2
- Outlines stay solid in front of water and light (also in Sly 3)
- Explosions from oil drums, crates and guards appear again (they went off far away)
- Sly can no longer climb the invisible pipes the Contessa uses (Episodes 4 and 5)
- Episode 7: job progress no longer goes back, and the bear cave's ice cracks make their noise again
- Episode 3: the water bug alerts the guards again
- Hacking: fire with the right stick again (advanced controls)
- The Controls page animates the controller you use from the start

## Android and Meta Quest
- **Turnip drivers** for Adreno GPUs (import a driver package in the launcher); a game that shows no picture goes back to the system driver by itself
- **Updates inside the launcher**, like on PC
- **Install, save and disc image locations** like on PC
- **Meta Quest 2** supported natively
- **Older Android versions** (Android 5 and newer); phones without Vulkan use OpenGL ES
- OpenGL ES works again for all three games, and the game no longer switches to OpenGL by mistake after being closed while starting
- The Back button closes a game cleanly
- Swipe to scroll in the launcher's pages

## All games
- **Freeze Camera** in the debug menu (Shift+F1): keep the view where it is and play on, either fixed or turning to follow you
- **Game time keys** in the debug menu: F8 freeze time, Shift+F8 faster, Ctrl+F8 slower
- Every platform now carries the launcher and all three games
